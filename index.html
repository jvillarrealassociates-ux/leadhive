FAQ

Common questions.

Can multiple agents work the same zip codes?

Yes — and that's by design. There's no limit on how many agents can subscribe to the same zip codes. But every lead is exclusive at the homeowner level. Once a homeowner is sent to any subscriber, they are permanently locked out of every other subscriber's feed — forever. Same zip, never the same person.

Where does the data come from?

County Appraisal District public property records (Bexar, Harris, and Nueces County). Every lead is a verified homeowner pulled from public records — no voter rolls or restricted data sources.

How do I receive my leads?

We share a Google Sheet with you. Every week, 15 fresh exclusive scored leads are added — date, score, grade, address, AI analysis, outreach scripts, and skip trace links.

How do I pick my zip codes?

After payment, reply to your receipt email with your 3 preferred zip codes from the available list. Your territory is locked in and your Sheet goes live within 24 hours.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Your territory reopens for the next subscriber.

How does property tax protest work as a niche?

Protest firms work on contingency — homeowners pay nothing upfront and only owe a percentage of savings if the protest succeeds. We score leads by year-over-year appraisal increase and absolute property value, and automatically filter out homeowners who already filed a protest. Leads are delivered April–July when Texas CADs mail appraisal notices. Many firms also use our list in the off-season (Aug–March) to sign clients early for the next protest cycle.

Is this data compliant for outreach?

All data comes from public property records. Always follow TCPA, RESPA (mortgage), CMS guidelines (Medicare), and applicable state regulations in your outreach. Scripts are written with compliance in mind.
