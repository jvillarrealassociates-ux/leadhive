FAQ

Common questions.

How many brokers share a territory?

Zero. Each territory is 100% exclusive. Once a set of zip codes is claimed, it's closed. We will never sell the same leads to two brokers.

Where does the data come from?

Bexar County Appraisal District public property records. Every lead is a verified homeowner with the Over-65 exemption on file — meaning they're confirmed 65+ and own their home outright or with significant equity.

How do I receive my leads?

We share a Google Sheet with you. Every morning, 15 fresh scored leads are appended to your sheet. You'll see the date, score, grade, address, AI analysis, and outreach scripts.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Your territory reopens for the next broker.

How quickly can I get started?

After payment you'll receive a receipt email — just reply with your 3 preferred zip codes. Your Google Sheet will be live and your territory locked in within 24 hours.

Are these compliant for reverse mortgage outreach?

All data comes from public property records — no voter rolls or restricted data sources. Always follow RESPA, TCPA, and applicable state regulations in your outreach.
